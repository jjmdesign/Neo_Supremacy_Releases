# Neo Supremacy Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Neo Supremacy is published by Redline Apps. This policy explains the information processed when you use the game and its online multiplayer features.

## Information the game processes

Neo Supremacy does not require a Redline Apps account. When you use online multiplayer, the game and the authoritative multiplayer server may process:

- the display name you choose;
- public and direct in-game chat messages, including their sender, recipient, and timestamp;
- game-session data, such as lobby membership, game settings, moves, saved match state, and reconnect credentials;
- technical connection and diagnostic information needed to operate and secure the service, such as connection identifiers, timestamps, errors, and server logs.

Reconnect credentials act as bearer credentials for your seat in a saved game. Keep them private. Do not share save files or diagnostic material that contains them.

## How information is used

This information is used only to:

- create, operate, save, resume, and troubleshoot multiplayer games;
- deliver public or direct chat messages to the intended participants;
- maintain service security, reliability, and abuse prevention;
- investigate reported bugs and support requests when you choose to provide diagnostic material.

Neo Supremacy does not use third-party advertising or behavioral advertising, and Redline Apps does not sell personal information.

## Visibility to other players

Your chosen display name and public chat messages are visible to other participants in the same game. Direct messages are shown only to their sender and intended recipient. Gameplay information is shared according to the game's fog-of-war and privacy rules.

## Storage and deletion

Online multiplayer session data and chat may be stored with the authoritative saved game so the game can be resumed after a server restart. Server logs may be retained for security, reliability, and troubleshooting. A game host can close and delete an eligible hosted game through the game interface. Local reconnect information can also be removed from the player's device by removing the game's local data.

## Security

Redline Apps uses reasonable technical and operational safeguards for multiplayer data. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not place sensitive personal information in a display name or chat message.

## Children's privacy

Neo Supremacy is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly request a child's real name, address, or other sensitive personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise online chat use as appropriate for the player's age.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the game or its online services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## Contact and privacy requests

For support, privacy questions, or deletion requests, open an issue in the public [Neo Supremacy release support repository](https://github.com/jjmdesign/Neo_Supremacy_Releases/issues). Do not include reconnect credentials, private chat, save files, or other sensitive information in a public issue. If sensitive diagnostic information is needed, request a private support channel first.
